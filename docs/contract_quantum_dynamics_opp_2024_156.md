2. **Service Level Agreement:** Vendor guarantees 99.95% uptime for the platform. Service credits will be provided for any downtime exceeding SLA thresholds, calculated as 5% of monthly fees for each hour of downtime.

6. **Intellectual Property:** Customer retains all rights to custom models and analytics developed using the platform. Vendor retains rights to platform technology and pre-built components.
